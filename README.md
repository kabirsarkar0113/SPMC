First version of massage company website:
inludes-
A single-page massage booking app built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no frameworks or dependencies.
Pick up to two massages per session (head, leg, hand, arm, foot, shoulder, back)
Choose a duration of 2 to 14 minutes with a slider
See an instant price at ₹5 per minute, per massage
Confirm a booking and view the transaction history with total revenue
Bookings are saved in the browser’s localStorage, so they persist across reloads
A “Pay” button clears the history once total revenue exceeds ₹100
Responsive layout that works on mobile
